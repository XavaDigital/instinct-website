## What's yours

Anything you bring to us stays yours: your club crest, sponsor logos, colours, and any pattern, motif or artwork that belongs to your group. The finished design we create around your artwork is yours to reorder for as long as you like, and we won't reproduce it for another customer.

For kapa haka rōpū, Pasifika and other cultural groups, this matters. Patterns with meaning to your group are treated as your property. We'll check placement with you before anything is printed and we won't use them in our portfolio without asking first.

## What's ours

Our base templates, garment patterns, generic graphic elements and design library remain the property of Instinct Apparel. You're free to reorder anything built from them, but they can't be passed to another supplier to reproduce.

## What you confirm when you send us artwork

By supplying artwork you confirm that:

- you have the right to use it, or the owner has given you permission;
- sponsor logos have been approved by the sponsor for use on your kit;
- nothing in the artwork infringes another party's trademark or copyright.

If we think a logo or image may be someone else's (a professional team's crest, a brand mark, a licensed character), we'll ask before we use it and may decline.

## How we store your files

We keep your artwork and final design files so you can reorder without re-briefing, and so late signings match the original run. Files are stored on secure cloud storage with access limited to our design and production staff. Ask at any time and we'll delete them.

## Showing our work

We like to show finished kit in our gallery and on social media. If you'd rather we didn't, tell us when you approve your mockup and we won't. We never include player names or personal details in what we publish.
