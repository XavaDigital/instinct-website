## Quotes and pricing

Every quote is in New Zealand dollars and shows GST separately. Quotes are valid for 30 days and are based on the quantities, garments and specifications you gave us — if those change, we'll reprice before production starts.

## Deposit

A deposit is due when you approve your final mockup; the amount is shown on your quote. Production is scheduled once the deposit has cleared, and the 4–6 week production time runs from that day.

## Balance

The balance, including freight, is due before dispatch. We'll send the invoice as your order goes through its final checks so there's no delay once it's ready.

## Ways to pay

- **Bank transfer** — account details are on every invoice. Please use your order number as the reference.
- **Card** — we can send a secure card payment link on request.
- **Individual player payments** — for larger orders we can issue individual payment links so each player or family pays for their own gear. Ask when you request a quote.

## Schools and clubs on account

Schools and established clubs can be invoiced on account with agreed payment terms once an account is set up. Send a purchase order number with your mockup approval if your school needs one on the invoice.

## Cancellations

Before approval, you can walk away at any time — quotes and mockups are free and carry no obligation. After approval, materials are ordered and printing begins, so cancelled orders are charged for work already completed. We'll tell you the amount before you confirm the cancellation.

## Overdue accounts

Overdue balances may incur interest and reasonable recovery costs. If a payment is going to be late, tell us — we'd much rather agree a plan than chase an invoice.
