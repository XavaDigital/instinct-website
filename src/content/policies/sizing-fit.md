## Sizing kits

A sizing kit is a set of sample garments in a range of sizes that we lend you so players can try before you confirm the size run. Ask for one when you request your quote. Kits are sent by tracked courier and we ask that they come back within two weeks so the next club can use them. Freight both ways is included for orders of 20 garments or more; for smaller orders we'll quote it.

## Size charts

Our [size charts](/size-charts) show garment measurements laid flat, along with the body size each is designed to fit. They're the reference for every order. If you're between sizes, size up for a relaxed fit or down for a tight fit — and tell us which cut you want, because tight-fit jerseys are cut closer than classic fit.

## Tolerance

Sublimated garments are cut and sewn individually, so small variation is normal. We work to a tolerance of ±2cm on chest, waist and length measurements against the published chart. A garment inside that range is made correctly.

## When we've got it wrong

If a garment is outside the tolerance, or is labelled and made to a different size from the one on your confirmed size run, that's on us. Tell us within 14 days of delivery with the order number and the measurements, and we'll remake it at no cost — usually ahead of other work so it reaches you quickly.

## When the size chosen was wrong

If a garment matches the chart and the size you confirmed but doesn't fit the player, we can't exchange it — it's custom made and can't be resold. What we can do is make a replacement in the right size at the same per-unit price as your order, with no minimum, and get it to you fast. Your design is on file, so it's a one-line email.

## Growing juniors

For junior teams we'll happily suggest a size spread that allows some growing room across a season, and we'll tell you where a size up is the safer choice.
