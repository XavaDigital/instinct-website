## What we can't take back

Every garment we make is sublimated and sewn for your order alone, so it has no value to anyone else. That means we can't accept returns or exchanges for:

- change of mind, or a decision to order fewer garments after approval;
- sizes that were confirmed on your size run but turned out to be wrong for the player;
- spelling, numbers, colours or logo placement that matched the mockup you approved;
- kit that matched the mockup you approved but doesn't meet a competition, league or governing-body uniform rule. We don't check designs against those rules — tell us the requirements and we'll build them into the mockup — but confirming compliance before you approve is the club's responsibility.

The mockup and size run are the checkpoint. Please check them carefully — we produce exactly what is approved.

## What we do cover

We stand behind our workmanship. If a garment has a manufacturing fault we'll put it right at no cost to you. Faults include:

- print defects such as banding, ghosting or misregistered panels;
- seams, hems, zips or drawcords that fail in normal use;
- a garment made to a size or specification different from the one you approved;
- measurements outside the tolerance in our [sizing and fit assurance policy](/policies/sizing-fit).

Normal wear, damage from incorrect washing (hot wash, bleach, tumble drying) and accidental damage aren't faults.

## How to raise a fault

1. Email us with your order number, the garment and size affected, a short description, and clear photos of the problem. Report anything visible on arrival — print defects, wrong sizes, wrong specification — within 14 days of delivery. Seams, zips and drawcords that fail in normal use during the first season are covered too; tell us as soon as it happens.
2. We'll reply within two business days. In most cases the photos are enough; occasionally we'll ask you to send the garment back, and we'll cover the courier.
3. We'll repair, remake or credit the affected garments — our choice, but we'll always talk it through with you first. Remakes are prioritised so they don't hold up your season.

## Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 where it applies. If you're buying for a business, the Act may not apply to your purchase.
