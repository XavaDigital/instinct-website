Shipping & delivery

Last updated 20 August 2026
In short

Orders ship tracked from Christchurch, usually 3–4 weeks from design approval. One club address is included in your quote; split-shipping to individual players is available for a per-parcel fee.

Dispatch times

Production begins once you approve your final mockup and your deposit clears. Standard production is 3–4 weeks; larger multi-grade orders may take longer and we will tell you before you approve. Rush production is sometimes possible for a surcharge — ask us early rather than late.

Rates and coverage

We deliver anywhere in New Zealand. Freight is quoted with your order so there are no surprises at invoice time.

DestinationTransitCost
Main centres1–2 working daysIncluded in quote
Rural delivery2–4 working daysRural surcharge applies
Split-ship to players1–4 working daysPer-parcel fee

Tracking, delays and lost parcels

You'll get a tracking link on dispatch. Once a parcel is with the courier, transit times are outside our control, but we will chase any delay on your behalf. If a parcel is confirmed lost we remake and resend the affected items at no cost to you.

Questions about a delivery in progress? Have your order number handy and give us a call.

1.3 "Mockup" means the visual proof we supply for your approval before production.

2. Quotes and pricing

2.1 Quotes are valid for 30 days from issue and are based on the quantities, garments and specifications you supplied.

2.2 Prices exclude GST unless stated otherwise. Freight is shown separately.

2.3 If quantities or specifications change after the quote is issued, we may reprice before production begins.

3.1 An order is confirmed when you approve the final mockup in writing and your deposit is received.

3.2 You are responsible for checking names, numbers, sponsor logos, spelling and sizes on the mockup. We produce exactly what is approved.

3.3 Changes requested after approval may incur additional cost and delay.

4. Artwork and intellectual property

4.1 By supplying artwork you confirm you have the right to use it and to authorise us to reproduce it.

4.2 You retain ownership of your logos and marks. We retain ownership of design templates and patterns we created.

4.3 We may show finished goods in our portfolio and marketing unless you ask us in writing not to.

5. Payment terms

5.2 Title in the goods passes to you on full payment. Overdue accounts may incur interest and recovery costs.

6. Production and delivery

6.1 Stated production times run from written mockup approval and cleared deposit, and are estimates in good faith.

6.2 Delivery terms are set out in our shipping policy, which forms part of these terms.

7. Sizing, variation and tolerances

7.1 Sizes follow our published charts. Sizing kits are available on request and we recommend using one.

7.2 Minor variation in measurements, colour and print placement is inherent to sublimation and is not a fault.

8. Faults, returns and remedies

8.1 Custom-made goods cannot be returned for change of mind or incorrect sizes selected by you.

8.2 Report any manufacturing fault within 14 days of delivery with photographs. We will repair, remake or credit at our option.

8.3 Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 where it applies.

9.1 Orders cancelled after mockup approval may be charged for work already completed, including materials and printing.

10. Liability

10.1 To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the value of the affected goods. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss.

11. Privacy

11.1 We handle your information in line with our privacy policy and the Privacy Act 2020.

12. Governing law

12.1 These terms are governed by New Zealand law and the New Zealand courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
